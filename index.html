GPS Essentilas SEA

GPS Essentilas SEA is a powerful offline GPS navigation app specially designed for fishermen.

Features:

  • Works completely offline (No internet required)
  • Accurate GPS location tracking
  • Waypoint saving and navigation
  • Designed for real sea usage

How it works:

Users can unlock the app by watching ads or purchasing a plan. Once unlocked, the app works fully offline without any interruption.

Download App:

Available on Google Play Store.
